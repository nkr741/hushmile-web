Hushmile privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026. Applies to the Hushmile Android app (app.hushmile.journey) and the web viewer at nkr741.github.io/hushmile-web. This is the privacy policy linked from Google Play.

In short

Hushmile lets someone share one road trip with the people they choose. A family journey needs no account. A travel company can create a desk account. Hushmile runs its own small server (api.hushmile.com) that relays encrypted updates and passes place search and traffic times to Google. It cannot read journey updates. Those updates are encrypted on the traveller's phone with a key made from the Journey Code and a random link key that travels only in the shared link. Only someone with that code can read them. We cannot read them. We do not sell personal data.

What the app uses

  • Precise location, only after the driver starts a journey and while it runs (shown by an ongoing notification). It stops when the journey ends or the code expires (18 hours).
  • The name the driver types, the start and destination they choose, vehicle type, planned route, battery level and whether it is charging, stops, kilometres covered and left, and check-in answers (All fine, Break, Traffic, Need help / SOS).
  • Only if the traveller adds them: in passenger mode, the cab's vehicle number, driver's name and cab app or company; and the traveller's own phone number saved in Settings for SOS, which is put in the encrypted update only while an SOS is on, so the family can call from the alert. Both are encrypted like everything else and are never part of the Journey Code.
  • The family phone sends only "seen" and "please check in" signals, also encrypted.

We do not use advertising, analytics, crash reporting or photos, and no phone number except the optional SOS number described above. The app cannot read your contacts list. If you tap Pick from contacts to share a journey, Android's own contact picker gives the app only the one name and number you tap; the app uses it on the phone to open WhatsApp or SMS with the share message and does not send it to us. On Android the app registers with Google Firebase Cloud Messaging when it starts. This gives the app a random installation ID and a push token that Google uses only to deliver wake-ups. They are not linked to your name or Journey Code, and uninstalling the app removes them from the phone.

Voice: read aloud and voice commands

  • Read aloud (the speaker button) uses the phone's own text-to-speech voice. It works without internet and sends nothing.
  • Voice commands (the mic button, in English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil or Kannada) use the microphone only after you tap the mic and allow it, and only while it is listening for one short command. The phone's speech recognition turns the sound into text on the phone; Hushmile then matches the text against a fixed list of commands on the phone. No sound and no text of what you said is sent to Hushmile, and nothing you say is recorded or kept.
  • Online speech is off unless you turn it on (Settings, Voice, Allow online speech). Only then, and only when the phone cannot understand your language offline, does your phone's speech service (usually Google) receive the sound of that one command to turn it into text, under that service's own privacy policy. Hushmile never sends your location, name or Journey Code with it.
  • If you choose Remember this phrase, the words are saved only on your phone, sealed like the app's other local data. Forget learned phrases in Settings deletes them.
  • Voice never sends an SOS or makes a call by itself. Every voice command is said back and waits for you to say yes or tap Yes; SOS then runs the same countdown with a Cancel button, and "call 112" only opens the dialler with the number filled in.

Travel desk

Drivers and families still do not sign in. A travel company that wants a booking list does. Creating that account sends us the company name, an email address and a password. We store the password only as a PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 hash with a random salt and 210,000 iterations. We never keep the password itself. The sign-in token is stored only as a SHA-256 hash and expires after 30 days.

A booking stores the vehicle number, the from and to text the company typed, who the trip is for (a cab, a school bus, a truck or an employee cab), an optional watcher name, the time they should start, and the status (not started, trip start not confirmed, on trip, location stale, arrived, or link stopped) with those times. It does not store the Journey Code or any location. The driver link does not contain the Journey Code. The office phone seals the code with the 4-digit number; the server keeps only that sealed box and cannot read the code. The company gets two different links. The driver link can start that one booking after the driver enters a 4-digit number, which is not inside the link. The server keeps only a hash of that number and a hash of the start secret. The watch link can only follow the journey. It cannot start it, end it, or open the company's other bookings. After the driver taps Start, the link they were sent can no longer end the trip. The phone that started it can. The owner or a dispatcher can stop one booking immediately. A dispatcher cannot delete the company.

A group code lets someone watch the bookings an office put in that group: the vehicle, the from and to text, the status, and the driver name the office typed. It cannot start or end a journey, and it does not open the encrypted map trail. The server stores only a hash of the code. The code lasts as long as the office set, at most 7 days, or until the office closes the group.

A booking key lets the company's own system create and list bookings. It cannot see a family's map, stop a trip, or delete the company. The server stores only a hash of the key. The office can stop a key in the app.

Security

  • The app allows only HTTPS. Journey updates use AES-256-GCM. The server carries the sealed message and cannot open it.
  • The Journey Code is not a field on the travel-desk API. A request that includes one is rejected.
  • Company passwords and session tokens are stored only as one-way hashes. Sign-up and sign-in are rate limited.
  • Deleting a company account requires the password again, so a saved session alone cannot wipe it.
  • We have not commissioned an independent security audit. These are the controls the software actually uses.

Who can see a journey

Anyone the driver gives the Journey Code to. They see the exact position on a map, its latitude and longitude, the nearest place name, battery level and whether it is charging, stops, time and distance left, and the last update time. Share the code only with people you trust.

Services the app talks to

  • Hushmile relay (api.hushmile.com, a self-hosted ntfy server in Bangalore, India) carries the encrypted updates. It keeps each sealed message for up to 19 hours and cannot read it. It sees the time and the phone's internet (IP) address, which it uses in memory for rate limits and does not write to its logs in normal operation.
  • ntfy.sh (public message relay) is the fallback when the Hushmile relay does not answer. It keeps each sealed message for about 12 hours and cannot read it. It sees the time and the phone's internet (IP) address.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging wakes the family phone when there is news. The wake-up carries only a random channel name and the words "New message": no place and nothing from the update. Google sees that the phone follows that channel and when it was woken.
  • Google Maps Platform (Places, Routes and the street name of where the phone is), reached only through the Hushmile server, for destination search, the address of this phone when a journey is started, and traffic-aware travel times. Google receives the text you type and the phone's position to name the street and rank results, the start and destination of the drive, and, while a journey runs, the phone's current position about every 15 minutes to update the time left. These requests come from the server's address, without your name, your phone's internet address or the Journey Code.
  • OpenStreetMap services (Nominatim and Photon for place search and place names, OSRM for driving routes and time, and OpenStreetMap map tiles). These receive the place you type or a position, over HTTPS, without your name or code, under their own privacy policies.
  • The map you see in the app. App builds that include a Google Maps key draw the map with the Google Maps SDK for Android: the phone fetches the map for the area on screen from Google, which also receives the usual device data its SDK sends, under Google's privacy policy. The route, the trail and the Journey Code are drawn on the phone and are not sent to Google. Builds without a key use OpenStreetMap map tiles instead.
  • Weak-signal stretches. The family's app (when it opens a journey) or this viewer (when the phone goes quiet) asks the Hushmile server which spots along the route often lose signal, so a gap there can read as expected. It asks for whole areas of about 40 x 20 km, never the route or the Journey Code. Only if the driver turns on Help map weak signal in Settings (off by default) does the end of a journey send one anonymous report: a list of squares of about 1 km where updates could not get out for 3 minutes or more, with the hour of the week and a rounded length. It has no code, name, exact times or order, leaves out everything within 2 km of the start and the end, and goes from the phone's internet address, which is used only for a daily limit. The server keeps only a count and usual gap per square, not the report, and shows a square only after 5 different journeys reported it.
  • Link key. Each journey has a random link key that travels only in the shared link (after the #) and is never sent to our server in readable form. A journey opened only through the link stores nothing about the key on the server. Only when someone follows with just the 8-character Journey Code does the traveller's phone store the link key on the Hushmile server, locked with a key made from the Journey Code by scrypt (a deliberately slow, memory-hard function) with a random salt for that journey. The server keeps only that locked copy, the salt, a lookup id made from the code the same slow way, and a check value for deleting it; it never holds a usable key. The locked copy is deleted when the journey ends and expires within 19 hours in any case. From app 1.6.6 the copy kept on the phone is sealed as well. It is not stored as a plain file. To be honest about the limit: a code-only journey is only as strong as the 8-character code, the slow lock, and the server's lookup limit for each internet address. Sharing the full link is stronger than reading out the code.
  • Only the traveller's phone can send updates. When a journey starts, the phone makes a private signing key that never leaves it and signs every point, route, alert and end inside the encryption. The link key is made from the matching public key, so the family app and this viewer can tell the traveller's updates from anyone else's and do not show the others. A watch link, its QR code or the Journey Code can only watch. To refuse such writes on our relay, the phone tells our server the public key and the names of the relay channels it writes; the server keeps them for 20 hours. That is not a code, a key that can unlock anything, or a location. Journeys started on app 1.6.8 or older are not covered.
  • Nearest town, stops and weather. While a journey runs, the app asks the Hushmile server for the nearest town (about every 5 km), what a stop of 5 minutes or more is at (fuel, food, a rest area or a toll plaza), and the weather (about every 30 minutes): heavy rain, fog, or visibility under 1 km. The server asks OpenStreetMap (Overpass, Nominatim) and Open-Meteo, and Google only if those do not answer, from its own address, with the position and nothing else. Answers are kept by area (about 1 to 11 km) so one answer serves many journeys. There is no flood map and no accident-prone map. The app does not invent those.
  • Hospitals and police on the route. When SOS is used, or someone taps Hospitals and police nearby, the app asks the Hushmile server for the nearest ones; the server asks OpenStreetMap (Overpass) from its own address with the position only, and keeps the answer by area (about 5 km) for a week. The web viewer instead offers a Google Maps search link near the last position, opened only if you tap it.
  • If the driver chooses to text someone or call for help, the phone's own SMS or dialler app is used. If the driver chooses to open the destination in Google Maps, the phone's Google Maps app is used.

This website and web viewer

The pages the app and Play open are the static pages at nkr741.github.io/hushmile-web, hosted on GitHub Pages. The website uses no cookies, analytics, trackers or third-party fonts. The Journey Code stays in the link after the #, which browsers do not send to the web server. The page decrypts updates in your browser and only connects to the Hushmile relay and ntfy.sh, and loads map tiles for the area of the route from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org), or OpenStreetMap on browsers without WebGL. They see your internet address and this site's name, never the code or the route itself. GitHub may log basic request data (such as IP address) under its own policy.

Keeping and deleting data

Journey details on the phone are kept only for the current journey and the last arrival screen. Ending a journey sends a "closed" notice so the code stops opening it, and relay copies expire after about 19 hours (12 hours on ntfy.sh). Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes everything on that phone, including a saved company sign-in.

A family journey is not linked to a name or email on our server. What the server keeps for it is encrypted messages that expire, a locked link key that expires, and anonymous weak-signal counts that are not tied to a person. A company account is linked to an email. Its bookings last 30 days, or until the company deletes the account in Travel desk. To ask us to delete anything else, email the address under Contact. We reply within 30 days, confirm what we hold, and delete what we find that is linked to you.

Children

Hushmile is meant for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Who operates this

The app and the server at api.hushmile.com are operated for Hushmile from India. The relay runs in Bangalore. Questions, access requests and deletion requests go to the contact below. This notice describes the data the software actually handles. It is the notice for the app, the web viewer and the travel desk.

Not an emergency service

Hushmile does not contact emergency services and never calls anyone by itself. SOS (hold the button for 3 seconds, then a 5 second countdown you can cancel) alerts the people following the journey through the same encrypted updates; it only works when the phone has signal, and nobody may see it in time. The app offers 112 in the phone's dialler; you press call. In an emergency call 112 (India) or your local number.

ನೀವು ಶುರು ಮಾಡೋ ಪ್ರಯಾಣದ ಸಮಯದಲ್ಲಿ, ಈ ಫೋನ್ ತನ್ನ ನಿಖರ ಲೊಕೇಶನ್ ಕಳಿಸುತ್ತೆ. ಪ್ರಯಾಣ ಕೋಡ್ ಇರುವ ಯಾರಾದರೂ ಮ್ಯಾಪ್‌ನಲ್ಲಿ ಸರಿಯಾದ ಜಾಗ, ಹತ್ತಿರದ ಊರು, ಉಳಿದ ದೂರ, ಸಮಯ, ಬ್ಯಾಟರಿ, ನಿಂತ ಜಾಗಗಳು, ಕೊನೆಯ ಅಪ್‌ಡೇಟ್ ಬಂದ ಸಮಯ ನೋಡ್ತಾರೆ. ಕ್ಯಾಬ್ ವಿವರಗಳು (ಪ್ರಯಾಣಿಕ ಮೋಡ್‌ನಲ್ಲಿ) ನೀವು ಸೇರಿಸಿದ್ರೆ ಮಾತ್ರ ಹೋಗುತ್ತವೆ; ನಿಮ್ಮ ಫೋನ್ ನಂಬರ್, SOSಗೆ ಸೇವ್ ಮಾಡಿದ್ದರೆ, SOS ಆನ್ ಇರುವಾಗ ಮಾತ್ರ. ಶುರು ಮಾಡೋ ಮುಂಚೆ ಏನೂ ಸಂಗ್ರಹಿಸಲ್ಲ; ಪ್ರಯಾಣ ಮುಗಿಸಿದಾಗ ಅಥವಾ ಕೋಡ್ ಅವಧಿ ಮುಗಿದಾಗ ನಿಲ್ಲುತ್ತೆ.

ಪ್ರಯಾಣ ಕೋಡ್ ಶುರುವಾದ 18 ಗಂಟೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಮುಚ್ಚುತ್ತೆ, ಅಥವಾ ನೀವು ಪ್ರಯಾಣ ಮುಗಿಸಿದಾಗ. ಆಗ ಫೋನ್‌ಗೆ ಸಿಗ್ನಲ್ ಇಲ್ಲದಿದ್ರೆ, ಮುಗಿಸಿದ್ದು ಸೇವ್ ಆಗಿ ಸಿಗ್ನಲ್ ಬಂದಾಗ ಕಳಿಸಲಾಗುತ್ತೆ. ಆಮೇಲೆ ಕೋಡ್ ಪ್ರಯಾಣವನ್ನು ತೆರೆಯಲ್ಲ. ಈಗಾಗಲೇ ಕಳಿಸಿದ ಎನ್‌ಕ್ರಿಪ್ಟ್ ಕಾಪಿಗಳು ಸುಮಾರು 19 ಗಂಟೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಸರ್ವರ್‌ಗಳಿಂದ ಅಳಿಸಲಾಗುತ್ತವೆ.

ಉಳಿದ ಸಮಯವನ್ನು ಕೊನೆಯ ಅಪ್‌ಡೇಟ್‌ನಿಂದ ಅಂದಾಜು ಮಾಡಲಾಗುತ್ತೆ; ಟ್ರಾಫಿಕ್ ಮಾಹಿತಿ ಇದ್ದರೆ ಅದರ ಜೊತೆ, ಇಲ್ಲದಿದ್ರೆ ಯೋಜಿಸಿದ ವೇಗದಿಂದ. ಫೋನ್‌ನಿಂದ ಏನೂ ಬರದಿದ್ರೆ ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯವಾಗಿ ಸಿಗ್ನಲ್ ಕಮ್ಮಿ ಅಂತ, ತುರ್ತು ಅಲ್ಲ. ‘ಸಹಾಯ ಬೇಕು’ ಮಾತ್ರ ಮನೆಯವರಿಗೆ ಏನಾದರೂ ಮಾಡೋಕೆ ಹೇಳೋ ಸ್ಟೇಟಸ್, ಅದೂ ಪ್ರಯಾಣಿಕರು ಅದನ್ನು ಟ್ಯಾಪ್ ಮಾಡಿದ್ರೆ ಮಾತ್ರ.

ಧ್ವನಿ: ಜೋರಾಗಿ ಓದೋದು ನಿಮ್ಮ ಫೋನ್‌ನ ಸ್ವಂತ ಧ್ವನಿ ಬಳಸುತ್ತೆ, ಇಂಟರ್ನೆಟ್ ಇಲ್ಲದೆಯೂ ಕೆಲಸ ಮಾಡುತ್ತೆ. ಧ್ವನಿ ಕಮಾಂಡ್‌ಗಳು ಫೋನ್‌ನಲ್ಲೇ ಇರುವ ಸ್ಪೀಚ್ ರೆಕಗ್ನಿಷನ್ ಬಳಸುತ್ತವೆ. ಸೆಟ್ಟಿಂಗ್ಸ್‌ನಲ್ಲಿ ಆನ್‌ಲೈನ್ ಸ್ಪೀಚ್ ನೀವು ಅನುಮತಿಸಿ, ಫೋನ್‌ಗೆ ನಿಮ್ಮ ಭಾಷೆ ಆಫ್‌ಲೈನ್‌ನಲ್ಲಿ ಆಗದಿದ್ದರೆ ಮಾತ್ರ, ಕಮಾಂಡ್‌ನ ಧ್ವನಿ ಪದಗಳಾಗಿ ಬದಲಾಗೋಕೆ ನಿಮ್ಮ ಫೋನ್‌ನ ಸ್ಪೀಚ್ ಸರ್ವೀಸ್‌ಗೆ (ಸಾಮಾನ್ಯವಾಗಿ Google) ಹೋಗುತ್ತೆ. ನೀವು ಹೇಳಿದ್ದನ್ನು Hushmile ಯಾವತ್ತೂ ರೆಕಾರ್ಡ್ ಮಾಡಲ್ಲ, ಇಟ್ಟುಕೊಳ್ಳಲ್ಲ, ಅದರ ಜೊತೆ ನಿಮ್ಮ ಲೊಕೇಶನ್ ಅಥವಾ ಪ್ರಯಾಣ ಕೋಡ್ ಕಳಿಸಲ್ಲ.

Hushmile ತುರ್ತು ಸೇವೆ ಅಲ್ಲ. ನೀವು ಟೈಪ್ ಮಾಡಿದ ಹೆಸರು ಕೋಡ್ ಜೊತೆ ಕಾಣುತ್ತೆ. ಪ್ರಯಾಣ ಸ್ಕ್ರೀನ್‌ನಿಂದ ಶೇರಿಂಗ್ ಮುಗಿಸಬಹುದು.

తెలుగు

Hushmile ఒకసారి ఒక ప్రయాణాన్నే షేర్ చేస్తుంది. కుటుంబ ప్రయాణానికి అకౌంట్ అవసరం లేదు. ట్రావెల్ కంపెనీ ఈమెయిల్, పాస్‌వర్డ్‌తో డెస్క్ అకౌంట్ తెరవవచ్చు. డ్రైవర్‌లు, కుటుంబాలు సైన్ ఇన్ చేయరు. జర్నీ కోడ్‌తో ఆ ఒక్క ప్రయాణాన్నే చూడగలరు. అది లాగిన్ కాదు, అందులో ఫోన్ నంబర్ ఉండదు.

మీరు మొదలుపెట్టిన ప్రయాణంలో ఈ ఫోన్ కచ్చితమైన లొకేషన్ పంపుతుంది. జర్నీ కోడ్ ఉన్నవారు మ్యాప్‌లో మీరు సరిగ్గా ఎక్కడున్నారో, దగ్గర్లోని ఊరు, మిగిలిన దూరం, సమయం, బ్యాటరీ, ఆగిన చోట్లు, చివరి అప్‌డేట్ సమయం చూడగలరు. క్యాబ్ వివరాలు (ప్యాసింజర్ మోడ్‌లో) మీరు ఇస్తేనే వెళ్తాయి. SOS కోసం ఫోన్ నంబర్ సేవ్ చేసి ఉంటే, SOS ఆన్‌లో ఉన్నప్పుడు మాత్రమే అది వెళ్తుంది. మొదలుపెట్టే ముందు ఏదీ తీసుకోం. ప్రయాణం ముగించినా, కోడ్ గడువు ముగిసినా ఇది ఆగిపోతుంది.

మొదలైన 18 గంటలకు, లేదా మీరు ప్రయాణం ముగించగానే జర్నీ కోడ్ పనిచేయడం ఆగిపోతుంది. అప్పుడు సిగ్నల్ లేకపోతే, ముగింపు ఈ ఫోన్‌లో సేవ్ అయి సిగ్నల్ వచ్చాక వెళ్తుంది. ఆ తర్వాత ఆ కోడ్‌తో ప్రయాణం తెరుచుకోదు. ఇప్పటికే పంపిన ఎన్‌క్రిప్ట్ చేసిన కాపీలు సుమారు 19 గంటల్లో సర్వర్ల నుంచి డిలీట్ అవుతాయి.

మిగిలిన సమయాన్ని చివరి అప్‌డేట్ నుంచి లెక్కిస్తాం; ట్రాఫిక్ సమాచారం ఉంటే దాంతో, లేకపోతే అనుకున్న స్పీడ్‌తో. ఫోన్ నుంచి అప్‌డేట్ రాకపోతే చాలాసార్లు సిగ్నల్ బలహీనంగా ఉండటమే కారణం, అది అత్యవసరం కాదు. కుటుంబాన్ని ఏదైనా చేయమని అడిగే స్టేటస్ ‘సహాయం కావాలి’ ఒక్కటే, అదీ ప్రయాణిస్తున్నవారు నొక్కినప్పుడే.

గొంతు, మాట: చదివి వినిపించడానికి మీ ఫోన్ సొంత గొంతు వాడుతుంది, ఇంటర్నెట్ లేకుండానే పనిచేస్తుంది. మాట ఆదేశాలు మీ ఫోన్‌లోని మాట గుర్తింపుతో ఫోన్‌లోనే అర్థమవుతాయి. మీరు సెట్టింగ్‌లలో ఆన్‌లైన్ మాట గుర్తింపుకి అనుమతి ఇచ్చి, మీ భాష ఫోన్‌కి ఆఫ్‌లైన్‌లో రాకపోతేనే, మీ మాట శబ్దం అక్షరాలుగా మారడానికి మీ ఫోన్ స్పీచ్ సర్వీస్‌కి (సాధారణంగా Google) వెళ్తుంది. మీరు అన్నది Hushmile రికార్డ్ చేయదు, ఎక్కడా ఉంచదు, దానితో మీ లొకేషన్ గానీ ప్రయాణ కోడ్ గానీ పంపదు.

Hushmile అత్యవసర సేవ కాదు. మీరు టైప్ చేసిన పేరు కోడ్‌తో పాటు కనిపిస్తుంది. ప్రయాణ స్క్రీన్ నుంచే షేరింగ్ ఆపవచ్చు.

हिन्दी

Hushmile एक बार में एक ही सफ़र शेयर करता है। परिवार के सफ़र के लिए अकाउंट नहीं चाहिए। ट्रैवल कंपनी ईमेल और पासवर्ड से डेस्क अकाउंट बना सकती है। ड्राइवर और परिवार साइन इन नहीं करते। जर्नी कोड से सिर्फ़ वही सफ़र दिखता है। यह लॉगिन नहीं है और इसमें फ़ोन नंबर नहीं होता।

आपके शुरू किए सफ़र में यह फ़ोन अपनी सटीक लोकेशन भेजता है। जर्नी कोड वाले मैप पर ठीक जगह, नज़दीकी शहर, बची दूरी और समय, बैटरी, रुकने की जगहें और आख़िरी अपडेट का समय देखते हैं। कैब की जानकारी (पैसेंजर मोड में) सिर्फ़ तभी भेजी जाती है जब आप उसे भरें, और SOS के लिए सेव किया गया फ़ोन नंबर सिर्फ़ SOS चालू रहने पर। शुरू करने से पहले कुछ नहीं लिया जाता, और सफ़र ख़त्म करने या कोड की मियाद ख़त्म होने पर यह रुक जाता है।

जर्नी कोड शुरू होने के 18 घंटे बाद, या सफ़र ख़त्म करने पर बंद हो जाता है। उस समय सिग्नल न हो तो सफ़र ख़त्म होने की ख़बर फ़ोन पर सेव रहती है और सिग्नल आने पर चली जाती है। उसके बाद उस कोड से सफ़र नहीं खुलता। भेजी जा चुकी एन्क्रिप्टेड कॉपियाँ लगभग 19 घंटे में सर्वर से डिलीट हो जाती हैं।

बचा समय आख़िरी अपडेट से आँका जाता है: ट्रैफ़िक की जानकारी हो तो उसके साथ, नहीं तो तय रफ़्तार से। फ़ोन से अपडेट न आए तो अक्सर वजह कमज़ोर सिग्नल होती है, इमरजेंसी नहीं। सिर्फ़ ‘मदद चाहिए’ वाला स्टेटस ही परिवार से कुछ करने को कहता है, और वह भी तभी जब सफ़र करने वाला उसे दबाए।

आवाज़: पढ़कर सुनाने के लिए आपके फ़ोन की अपनी आवाज़ इस्तेमाल होती है, और यह बिना इंटरनेट के चलता है। आवाज़ से काम आपके फ़ोन की आवाज़ पहचान से फ़ोन पर ही समझे जाते हैं। सिर्फ़ तब, जब आप सेटिंग्स में ऑनलाइन आवाज़ पहचान की इजाज़त दें और फ़ोन आपकी भाषा ऑफ़लाइन न समझ पाए, आपकी बात की आवाज़ शब्दों में बदलने के लिए आपके फ़ोन की स्पीच सर्विस (आमतौर पर Google) को जाती है। Hushmile आपकी बात न रिकॉर्ड करता है, न रखता है, और उसके साथ आपकी लोकेशन या जर्नी कोड कभी नहीं भेजता।

Hushmile इमरजेंसी सेवा नहीं है। आपका लिखा नाम कोड के साथ दिखता है। सफ़र वाली स्क्रीन से शेयरिंग बंद कर सकते हैं।
